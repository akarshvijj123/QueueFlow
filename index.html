Built for High-Footfall Operations

Key features designed to reduce waiting room congestion and improve service speed.

Digital Tokens

Give customers a clear digital token accessible on any phone screen without paper tickets.

Live Queue Tracking

Customers can see their current position and how many people are ahead in real-time.

Estimated Waiting Time

Predictive time estimates help customers understand how long they may wait before service.

Turn Notifications

Notify customers with clear visual badges when their turn is approaching or called.

Queue Management

Staff can call next, pause, resume, and control service counters easily with one click.

Service Analytics

Organizations gain insights into peak hours, average wait times, and daily throughput.
